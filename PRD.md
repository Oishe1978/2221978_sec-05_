Product Requirements Document (PRD)

1. Project Title
Dengue Dashboard & Analytics System

2. Executive Summary
The Dengue Dashboard is a web-based analytics platform designed to monitor, analyze, and visualize dengue outbreak data. The system enables public health officials and users to track infection counts, geographical hotspots, and trend predictions to improve resource allocation and preventive measures.

3. Problem Statement
Dengue outbreaks present recurring health threats. Lack of centralized real-time tracking and accessible visual data makes it difficult for authorities and citizens to identify high-risk areas promptly.

4. Objectives & Goals
- Provide real-time reporting and visual analytics for dengue cases.
- Identify geographic dengue outbreak hotspots.
- Deliver predictive insights for upcoming outbreak trends.
- Enable user-friendly data navigation via clean interactive dashboards.

5. Target Audience
- Public Health Authorities: To monitor trends and distribute healthcare resources.
- General Citizens: To check risk levels in their surrounding areas.

6. Key Features
- Interactive Overview Dashboard: Summary cards showing total cases, active cases, recoveries, and deaths.
- Geographic Mapping: Visual map indicating high-risk zones (hotspots).
- Historical Trend Charting: Line charts for tracking monthly/yearly dengue cases.
- Data Export: Capability to download reports in JSON/CSV formats.
